THE THINKING
BEHIND REV

REV Improvement Consulting was founded on a simple principle: South African businesses deserve access to the same calibre of strategic thinking that powers global enterprises.

We combine boardroom-tested experience with a growing network of specialist partners to deliver outcomes, not theory.

That principle is led by founder Ryan van Es, a C-suite executive and strategic operator with over 13 years of hands-on leadership across South Africa's most demanding industries.

He has built businesses from the ground up, negotiated landmark deals, turned around failing operations, and led organizations through crisis — all with measurable, documented results.
