Accounting Software
Xero

Cloud-based accounting software for small and medium businesses, handling invoicing, bank reconciliation, and financial reporting.

Why REV recommends it: We run our own books on Xero and have set up and implemented it for several client businesses — our go-to when a client needs clean, reliable financial visibility without an enterprise-level system.

Visit Xero →
YO
POS & Payments
YOCO

South African point-of-sale and payments platform built for small merchants, from card readers to online checkout.

Why REV recommends it: We've integrated YOCO for Shopify and e-commerce clients and installed it for small merchant vendors — a fast, low-friction way to start taking payments without heavy setup overhead.

Visit YOCO →
SP
Payroll
SimplyPay

Cloud payroll software built for South African compliance (PAYE, UIF, SDL) at a startup-friendly price point.

Why REV recommends it: We set this up as the payroll solution for the Legacy MVNO build — simple and cost-effective for a growing business that doesn't need a heavyweight HR/payroll suite yet.

Visit SimplyPay →
PF
Payment Gateway
PayFast

South African online payment gateway connecting e-commerce stores to local banks.

Why REV recommends it: Implemented as POS and online payment integration for e-commerce stores and portals, linking through to various local RSA banks.

Visit PayFast →
CD
Credit Bureau
Cred-it-data

NCR-registered South African credit bureau (license NCRCB25) offering pay-per-use business and consumer credit vetting, pulling comparative reports from TransUnion, Experian, XDS, and SAFPS.

Why REV recommends it: Low barrier to entry with easy sign-up and no subscription fees, but still delivers high-level, granular reporting — a good fit for clients who need proper credit vetting without an enterprise contract.

Visit Cred-it-data →
