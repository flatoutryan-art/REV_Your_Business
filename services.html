What We Do

WHAT IS AI STRATEGY & MANAGEMENT CONSULTING?

AI strategy and management consulting is the process of working with an experienced strategic operator to evaluate your business operations, identify where artificial intelligence and process improvement will move the needle fastest, and build a practical, executable plan to get there. Unlike generic technology advice, it starts from the commercial reality of the business — revenue, cost, risk, and people — and works backward to the right intervention, whether that's an AI adoption roadmap, an operational turnaround, or C-suite advisory during a period of change.

REV Improvement Consulting delivers this across four capability streams for South African businesses in Johannesburg, Sandton, and beyond: Revenue & Growth, Efficiency & Operations, Vision & Leadership, and AI-Augmented Strategy. Most engagements draw on more than one stream, because real business challenges rarely live in a single box.
